This Wednesday in the build-up to the Copa del Rey game with Real Betis, the FC Barcelona players were honoured for winning the Spanish Super Cup, and especially the extraordinary way in which they achieved it. Three days after triumphing 5-2 against Real Madrid, the Estadi Olimpic got the chance to celebrate together the first trophy of 2025. Ronald Araujo – surrounded by the starting XI from Sunday – was the man who got to lift the cup before the fans, and also the mascot CAT, who was naturally in attendance. Real Betis joined the occasion by welcoming the blaugrana players onto the field with a guard of honour.
